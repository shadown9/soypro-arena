Responsible gaming

Last updated: October 10, 2026

We want ProArena to be fun, not a worry. A few tips and tools:

Your time

  • Matches last 1 to 3 minutes: set a limit of matches or time before you start.
  • Take breaks. If you play at night, mind your rest.

Your purchases

  • Playing is free and buying gives no advantage: everything for sale is cosmetic.
  • You see exactly what you buy before paying; there are no loot boxes and no betting.
  • Gems have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Parents and guardians: Google Play and the App Store let you require a password or your approval for every purchase.

Respect

If someone bothers you, let us know at soyprollc1@gmail.com. We may suspend anyone who harasses other players.

Need to talk to someone?

If you feel screen time is affecting you, talk to someone you trust or to a health professional.
