Responsible gaming

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Playing should be entertainment, not a way to make money or recover losses.

Tools in your account

  • Deposit limits daily, weekly and monthly. Lowering them applies immediately; raising or removing them takes effect after 24 hours.
  • Self-exclusion for 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 6 months or indefinitely. While it lasts you cannot deposit or play with real money, and it cannot be shortened.
  • Time reminder after every hour of continuous play.

Find them in Profile → Account → Responsible gaming.

Warning signs

  • You play longer or spend more than you intended.
  • You try to win back losses.
  • It affects your work, studies, sleep or family.
  • You hide it or get irritable when you cannot play.

Get help

If any of these sound familiar, use self-exclusion and seek support. In the US: 1-800-GAMBLER (national helpline). In other countries, check your local support service. You can also write to us: soyprollc1@gmail.com.

Minors

Real-money play is for adults only. If a minor uses your device, protect your account and payment methods.
