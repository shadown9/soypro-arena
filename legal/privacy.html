Controller

Soy Pro LLC, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of New Jersey (USA), located at 81 Putnam St, Paterson, NJ 07524. Contact: soyprollc1@gmail.com.

What data we process

  • Account: player name, email, password (stored hashed with scrypt; we never see it), date of birth and region.
  • Gameplay: results, scores, rating and statistics.
  • Money: balance transactions. Card and bank details are handled directly by Stripe; we never receive them.
  • Technical: IP address and input patterns during matches, for security and anti-cheat.

Why we use it

To provide the service, process payments, meet legal obligations (age, region, accounting, fraud and money-laundering prevention) and ensure fair play. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Stripe (payments and identity checks for withdrawals), the email provider (notices) and the hosting provider (Fly.io). Only what each service needs.

How long

While you have an account. When you delete it we erase your personal data; accounting records are kept anonymized for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data (Profile and Account in the app) and write to soyprollc1@gmail.com for any request. Depending on your country, you may complain to your data protection authority.

Minors

Real-money play is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from minors to play for money.

On-device storage

We store your session, language and sound settings on your device. We do not use tracking cookies.
