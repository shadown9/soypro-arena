Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Controller

Soy Pro LLC, a limited liability company formed in the State of New Jersey (USA). Contact: contacto@soyproarena.com.

What data we process

  • Account: player name, avatar and, if you create an email account, your email and password (stored hashed; we never see it).
  • Game: results, scores, level, rank, badges and the items you own.
  • Purchases: what you bought, when and the amount. Card data is handled directly by Stripe, Google or Apple; we never receive it.
  • Technical: a device identifier and IP address, for account security (alerting you if someone logs in from another phone) and anti-cheat.
  • Notifications (only if you turn them on): a notification identifier provided by your phone (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android; Apple's or your browser's push service on iPhone and web). We use it only to tell you about game invites, friend requests, replies from the team and ProArena news. You can turn them off anytime in the app or phone settings, and it is erased when you delete your account.

Why we use it

So you can play, keep your progress, receive what you buy, protect your account and keep the game fair. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising. The app has no ads.

Who we share it with

Only with those needed to run the service: the payment processor (Stripe, Google Play or App Store), the email provider that sends you notices, and Cloudflare, which protects the connection to our servers.

How long

While you have an account. When you delete it we erase your personal data; payment records are kept anonymized for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can view and correct your data in the app, delete your account from Profile → Account, or write to contacto@soyproarena.com for any request, including deleting your data without opening the app.

Children

ProArena is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security and on-device storage

Connections are encrypted. On your device we store your session, language and settings. We do not use tracking cookies.
