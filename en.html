FAQ

Is this gambling or a casino?

No. These are skill competitions: the best player wins (reflexes, strategy, decisions). No slots, roulette, cards or lotteries, and match conditions are the same for everyone.

Who do I play against in prize matches?

Only real people of a similar level. Prize matches have no bots and no automatic assistance.

How much does it cost?

Free play costs nothing. Prize matches have a $1, $5 or $10 entry; the pool goes to the top players, minus a 10% fee.

How do I deposit and withdraw?

From the wallet in the app: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and more, through Stripe. Unplayed money goes back to the same payment method; prizes go to your bank or debit card.

Can I set limits?

Yes: deposit limits, breaks and self-exclusion from the app, whenever you want.
