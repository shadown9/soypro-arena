FAQ

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Every game, every map and the seasons are free. Optionally you can buy gems for skins or the premium season pass.

Does paying give an advantage?

No. Everything for sale is cosmetic: colors, skins, effects and badges. In a match everyone plays under the same conditions.

Is there betting or cash prizes?

No. ProArena has no betting, casino, lottery or cash prizes. You play for fun, for your rank and for badges.

Who do I play against?

Real players, live. If a match is short of players we fill it with game-controlled rivals so you never have to wait. Real-money matches are always people only.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, anytime, from your Profile in the app. Your data is deleted.
