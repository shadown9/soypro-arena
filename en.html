The games

🎯 Blind Spot

Tap the targets before they vanish and dodge the bombs. Pure reflex. Free play only.

🐍 Worm Arena

Eat to grow, dodge the others and make them crash into you.

🟩 Territory

Leave your zone, draw a loop and claim ground before someone cuts your line.

🏰 Conquest

Bases that produce troops. Attack, defend and rule the map.

🛡️ Zone

Tanks in a closing zone. Last one standing wins.

🫧 Absorption

Eat smaller cells, split to hunt and run from the big ones.

How it works

  1. Free: play without paying, level up, unlock maps and skins.
  2. Real money: pay a $1, $5 or $10 entry and get matched only with real players of a similar level, on the same kind of device and with a good connection. No bots.
  3. Prizes: the pool is the sum of the entries minus a 10% platform fee. Before the match you choose how it is split: among the top 3 (top 2 with 3 or 4 players) or winner takes all. Prizes are always whole dollars and are shown before you pay.
  4. Withdrawals: your winnings are transferred to your bank account through Stripe.

Fair and responsible play

  • Skill only: no chance, no casino, no lottery.
  • To play for money: 18+, verified email, trusted phone and an allowed region.
  • Fair skill-based matchmaking and server-side anti-cheat.
  • Deposit limits, breaks and self-exclusion whenever you need them.
  • Payments securely processed by Stripe. We never store your card details.

Real-money matches are not available in Arizona, Arkansas, Connecticut, Delaware, Louisiana, Montana, South Carolina, South Dakota and Tennessee, or anywhere local law does not allow them. You can always play for free there.

FAQ

Is this gambling or a casino?

No. These are skill competitions: the outcome depends on how you play (reflexes, strategy, decisions), not on chance. There are no slots, roulette, cards or lotteries. Match conditions are the same for every player.

Who do I play against when I pay an entry?

Only real people of a similar level. Real-money matches have no bots and no automatic assistance.

What do I need to play for money?

Be 18 or older, verify your email, use your trusted phone and be in an allowed region. Each account is personal and can only be used from one phone.

How do I deposit and withdraw?

From the wallet in the app, with card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or other methods offered by Stripe. Withdrawals go to your bank account.

Can I set limits?

Yes. You can set deposit limits, take breaks or self-exclude for as long as you want from the app.
